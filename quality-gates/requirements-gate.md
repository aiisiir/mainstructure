# Quality Gate: Requirements

A project exits requirements only when:
- [ ] Goals, non-goals, users, and decision owner are explicit.
- [ ] High-priority requirements have testable acceptance criteria.
- [ ] Constraints, assumptions, and open decisions are distinguished.
- [ ] Security, privacy, accessibility, reliability, performance, and operations are considered.
- [ ] Public web/SEO applicability, route families, languages, and content ownership are recorded.
- [ ] Material risks have owners and mitigation plans.
- [ ] Requirements-to-verification traceability exists for critical behavior.
