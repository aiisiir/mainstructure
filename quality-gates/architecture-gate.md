# Quality Gate: Architecture and Stack

- [ ] Project profile and hard constraints are reviewed.
- [ ] Viable options and trade-offs are documented; score weights were agreed before scoring.
- [ ] Selected stack has an ADR and operational ownership plan.
- [ ] System/data/trust boundaries and authorization are clear.
- [ ] Failure modes, integration timeouts/retries, consistency, idempotency, and migrations are considered.
- [ ] Security, privacy, performance, accessibility, and maintainability trade-offs are addressed.
- [ ] Public web rendering, URL/language, crawl strategy and SEO are decided or explicitly deferred with a blocker/owner.
- [ ] Deploy, observe, backup/restore, and rollback are feasible.
- [ ] High-impact assumptions have evidence or a time-boxed spike.
