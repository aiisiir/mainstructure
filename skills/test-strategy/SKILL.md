# Test Strategy Skill

Map critical requirements and risks to the cheapest effective verification layer. Define unit, integration, contract, E2E, security, accessibility, performance, migration, and recovery checks as relevant. Prioritize negative cases, permission failures, duplicates/retries, concurrency, and downstream failures. Identify flakiness and environmental assumptions. Distinguish automated tests from manual checks and untested risk. For indexable routes, include HTML/render, status, canonical/hreflang, robots/sitemap, redirect, structured-data, and performance checks.
