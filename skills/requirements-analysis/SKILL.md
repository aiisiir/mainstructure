# Requirements Analysis Skill

1. Read the project brief and constraints.
2. Separate fact, assumption, hypothesis, and unknown.
3. Identify actors, goals, workflows, domain rules, permissions, failure states, and non-goals.
4. Write functional and non-functional requirements with IDs, priority, rationale, and acceptance criteria.
5. Include security, privacy, accessibility, performance, operations, and SEO/discoverability requirements where relevant.
6. Check for contradictions, vague adjectives, missing edge cases, and untestable claims.
7. Produce an open-questions log and requirement-to-test mapping.

Do not invent answers to unresolved product questions. Propose options and identify who must decide.
