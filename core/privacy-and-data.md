# Privacy and Data Handling

- Inventory personal and sensitive data before designing storage or integrations.
- Collect only data justified by a documented purpose; define access, retention, deletion, export, and correction behavior where relevant.
- Define data classification and handling rules; minimize propagation into logs, analytics, AI prompts, and third-party services.
- Assess data residency, cross-border processing, contracts, and applicable legal requirements with qualified owners; do not infer legal compliance from a technical checklist.
- Protect backups and exports at least as carefully as primary data.
- Use synthetic or minimized data in development and testing wherever feasible.
- Document processor/vendor roles and what data is sent to external services.
