# Workflow: Feasibility Review

1. Review scope, deadline, budget, skill availability, hosting and operational ownership.
2. Assess technical options, integrations, data migration, dependencies, security/privacy, accessibility, performance, and SEO if applicable.
3. Identify the top risks and assumptions that can invalidate the plan.
4. Compare buy/build/reuse options and the total cost of ownership, including support and exit cost.
5. Propose spikes with explicit questions and timeboxes for high-impact uncertainty.
6. Recommend proceed, proceed with conditions, spike, reduce scope, choose alternative, or stop.
7. Capture conditions, owners, and decision date.

**Exit condition:** a named owner accepts the feasibility assessment and remaining risks.
