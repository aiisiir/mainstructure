# Workflow: Discovery

**Goal:** turn a broad request into a decision-ready problem statement.

1. Read the request and existing evidence. Do not rewrite assumptions as facts.
2. Identify users, problem, current workaround, desired outcomes, and measurable success indicators.
3. Define in-scope and out-of-scope items, constraints, stakeholders, and decision owner.
4. Classify user journeys and public/private surfaces. If public web content exists, record search intent, language, content ownership, and SEO importance.
5. Create an assumptions/unknowns log. Rank unknowns by impact and uncertainty.
6. Recommend research or a time-boxed spike only when its outcome could change a decision.
7. Produce `project-brief.md` and a list of unresolved decisions.

**Exit condition:** stakeholders can explain the problem and how success will be evaluated; material contradictions are recorded.
